About

DozerNet

A web-based JCB management system that replaces phone-based coordination with a transparent, centralised platform connecting customers, private owners, operators and administrators.

The problem

Bookings, maintenance, operator and payment records sit in different places, coordinated over calls and messages — slow and error-prone.

Our solution

One verified platform with shared, up-to-date information for every stakeholder, built on solid object-oriented design.
